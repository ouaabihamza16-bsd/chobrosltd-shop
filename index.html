About us

A small property company rooted in Manchester

CHO BROS LTD is an independent, privately owned property company based in Manchester. It was incorporated in England and Wales on 13 March 2024 and is registered at Companies House under company number 15560618.

Our business is straightforward. We buy residential property for our own portfolio, hold it as a long-term investment, and from time to time sell property we own. Everything we buy and sell is on our own account.

We aren't an estate agent, a letting agent or a managing agent, and we don't act for other people's property. We keep things plain and direct, the way we'd want to be dealt with ourselves.

What we do

Buying, owning and selling our own property

Our work covers three parts of a property's life with us, from the day we buy it to the day we decide to sell.

Property acquisition

We buy residential property in Manchester for our own portfolio. We look at each home on its own merits and consider whether it's right for us to own over the long term.

If you're selling a home, or you're an agent acting for a seller, you're welcome to write to us.

Long-term ownership

We hold the property we buy as a long-term investment, not as a quick turnaround. As the owner, we take responsibility for the homes in our portfolio.

Being small and privately owned means decisions are made directly, without layers in between.

Selling our own property

From time to time we sell property from our own portfolio. When we do, we're the seller, dealing as the owner of the home.

We only sell property we own. We don't market, sell or let property for anyone else.
